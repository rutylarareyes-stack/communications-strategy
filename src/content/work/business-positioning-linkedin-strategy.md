### What Needs to Happen Before an Entrepreneur Starts Posting on Social Media?

**Before publishing a single post, an entrepreneur must define their value proposition, pricing structure, and target positioning—including how they present their authority on LinkedIn.** Having strong expertise or a great product does not automatically translate into consistent client inquiries when messaging is scattered across disconnected online and offline channels.

On platforms like **LinkedIn**, experts often post without a clear positioning angle—making it difficult for ideal clients, partners, and search engines to recognize their authority.

### How Does Strategic Positioning Connect Business Offers With Client Communication?

**Effective communication starts before the first social media post by aligning business objectives, pricing structure, and audience research with clear messaging.** Working collaboratively alongside founders and independent professionals—including projects such as **EDV Garden** (a creative garden and visitor experience in **Florence**) and personal brand development for **Kathryn Clairand** (an independent English educator based in **France**)—I build practical strategic foundations tailored to each market.

Core consultancy pillars include:

* **Business, Market & Competitor Analysis:** Evaluating business goals, target audiences, market context, and competitors to uncover clear positioning gaps and differentiation opportunities.
* **Value Proposition & Brand Positioning:** Defining what makes a business or professional offer distinct, isolating Unique Selling Points (USPs), and translating them into clear, persuasive core messaging.
* **Pricing Strategy & Offer Development:** Advising on pricing structures and value-communication techniques so entrepreneurs package their services clearly and price in line with market positioning.
* **LinkedIn Personal Branding & Profile Strategy:** Conducting LinkedIn profile audits, benchmark analyses, and audience research to establish recognizable positioning, content pillars, visual guidelines, and editorial plans.
* **Integrated Online & Offline Communications:** Aligning brand messaging across websites, LinkedIn, social media, email marketing, and physical touchpoints such as printed leaflets and visitor materials.
* **Editorial Planning & Content Execution:** Turning strategic positioning into practical storytelling frameworks and repeatable content roadmaps that support sustainable commercial growth.
