---

### The Challenge

Following a move to a larger clinic, an established dental practice in Emilia-Romagna needed to rebuild its local visibility and attract new patients while preserving its reputation for high-quality care.

Despite upgrading its physical space, the practice had an underdeveloped digital presence. Online touchpoints were underutilized, brand messaging lacked consistency, and there was no dedicated budget for paid advertising. The goal was to increase local awareness, strengthen patient trust, and reach new families through a sustainable, organic communications system.

---

### Strategic Approach & Execution

Starting with a full digital audit, I designed a tailored local communications roadmap and worked directly alongside the clinic team to build their local search presence, social channels, and patient retention workflow.

* **Local SEO & Google Reputation Management:** Audited and optimized the clinic's **Google Business Profile** with updated location data, service categories, and structured business details. Designed a repeatable patient review-generation workflow that the clinic team implemented alongside fresh clinic photography.
* **Organic Social Media Strategy (Facebook & Instagram):** Built the strategic launch plan for the clinic's Facebook page alongside an Instagram editorial calendar focused on human storytelling, clinical expertise, and introducing the team.
* **Specialized Pediatric Service Positioning:** Developed dedicated visual branding and messaging materials for the clinic's children's dentistry services, helping the practice stand out to local families.
* **Patient Retention & Newsletter System:** Designed an email newsletter workflow to keep existing and former patients informed about clinic updates, preventive care, and seasonal initiatives—driving repeat visits and word-of-mouth referrals.

---

### Results & Business Impact

* **4.9/5 Google Reputation (172 Reviews):** Established strong local search credibility that reassures prospective patients before they ever call the clinic.
* **Cohesive Local Digital Ecosystem:** Connected Google Business Profile visibility, Instagram/Facebook storytelling, and email follow-ups into one unified patient journey.
* **Clearer Family & Pediatric Positioning:** Gave the clinic dedicated communication tools to engage parents and grow its children's dentistry patient base.
* **Repeatable Team Workflow:** Equipped the clinic staff to maintain review requests and patient updates consistently without external ad spend.

---

### Key Takeaway

For local healthcare practices and service SMEs, sustainable growth does not require expensive ad campaigns. Connecting accurate local search visibility, systematic review generation, human-centered social storytelling, and patient email communication builds lasting community trust that compounds over time.
